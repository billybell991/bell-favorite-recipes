## Ingredients

- For the Broth:
- 1 Rotisserie Chicken Carcass (meat stripped and set aside)
- 1 Onion, halved
- 4 Garlic cloves, smashed
- 1 tbsp Apple Cider Vinegar
- 1 shot (approx. 1 tbsp) Soy Sauce
- 1 shot (approx. 1 tbsp) Worcestershire Sauce
- 1/2 tsp Cayenne Pepper
- Crushed Red Pepper Flakes (to taste)
- Water (to the Max Fill line)
- To Finish the Soup:
- 1 can Corn Niblets, drained
- 1 can Stewed Whole Tomatoes, cut or crushed into bite-sized pieces (with juice)
- 2 clumps Egg Noodles
- Salt and Black Pepper (be generous!)
- Fresh Basil or Oregano (optional, chopped)

## Instructions

1. Pressure Cook: Place the carcass, onion, garlic, vinegar, soy sauce, Worcestershire, cayenne, and red pepper flakes into your Instant Pot. Fill with water to the max line.
2. The Timer: Secure the lid. Set to High Pressure for 60 minutes.
3. The Wait: When the time is up, let the pressure release naturally for 20 minutes before opening the lid.
4. The Strain: Set a colander over a large pot or bowl and pour everything through. Keep the liquid; discard the bones, spent onion, and garlic.
5. Reheat: Pour the strained broth back into the Instant Pot and set it to Sauté.
6. Add the Goods: Stir in the cut-up tomatoes (and their juice) and the drained corn. Add any leftover chicken meat you saved from the initial cleaning.
7. Seasoning: This is the most important part. Add a "decent bunch" of salt and pepper. Taste it—the corn and tomatoes are sweet, so keep adding salt and pepper until the flavors really pop.
8. Noodles: Add your egg noodles to the boiling soup and cook for 6–8 minutes until tender.
